Transform the source text into an outrageously surreal, ludicrous and funny version.

The result must preserve the CORE MEANING and broad sequence of events from the original, but the language itself should be radically transformed.

The reader should just about be able to recognise what the original was about, while wondering what the hell happened to it.

LENGTH:
- Keep the rewritten extract approximately the same length as the original.
- Aim for roughly 90% to 115% of the original word count.
- Do NOT make the rewrite twice as long as the source.
- Do not add lengthy new scenes, backstory, explanations or tangents.
- Create absurdity primarily by REPLACING existing material rather than continually adding new material.
- If you add a ridiculous detail, compensate by shortening or replacing something elsewhere.

TRANSFORMATION STRENGTH:
- Rewrite aggressively.
- Do NOT simply copy the original sentences and insert jokes into them.
- Change the sentence structures and phrasing substantially.
- Replace MOST names with supplied names.
- Replace MOST places with supplied places.
- Replace MOST significant nouns with supplied or invented absurd nouns.
- Replace MOST adjectives and adverbs with supplied or invented ridiculous alternatives.
- Replace distinctive imagery, metaphors and descriptions with surreal alternatives.
- Avoid retaining memorable phrases from the source.
- Ordinary connecting words can remain where needed for grammar and readability.
- Preserve the basic event or idea, NOT the original vocabulary.

The finished version should feel like somebody has taken the original through a literary blender rather than merely decorating it with jokes.

COMEDY STYLE:
- Treat completely ridiculous events with absolute seriousness.
- Use deadpan delivery. Never acknowledge that anything is unusual.
- Use bizarre specificity.
- Give people ridiculous motivations and unjustified confidence.
- Introduce stupid consequences that nevertheless follow their own strange logic.
- Use unexpected comparisons and surreal imagery.
- Let absurd situations escalate rapidly.
- Prefer surprising, sharp jokes over generic randomness.
- Occasionally use an oddly specific mundane detail in the middle of something spectacularly ridiculous.
- Characters may behave as though insane decisions are completely sensible.
- Avoid explaining jokes.
- Avoid saying that something is "absurd", "crazy", "surreal" or "ridiculous". Simply present it as fact.
- Keep enough narrative coherence that the nonsense has a thread.
- Aim for repeated "what the fuck?" moments while remaining readable.
