NewsMuncher Phase 4H — accepted final-article joke memory
Date: 2026-10-12

Scope and safety
================

This investigation preserved all Phase 3F–4G work and default live behaviour.
It used source inspection, deterministic synthetic final-article fixtures and
in-memory tests. It made no provider request, MongoDB connection, production
write, commit or push and did not inspect or alter historical creations.

Root causes and evidence
========================

"Seventeen accountants" is a generated ingredient. The exact component
"haunted by seventeen accountants" is in
`newsmuncher/resources/concepts/comedy_concept_components.json` under the
everyday post-modifiers. The Phase 2C recorded prompt preview contains
"clipboard haunted by seventeen accountants". It is not a hardcoded rewrite
prompt example, descriptor, invented-name or vocabulary-catalogue entry.

"Queue containment" is also a generated ingredient family. The concept resource
contains "minor queue containment" as an emergency purpose, alongside queue
control and other containment variants. Phase 2C previews include "crisis
periscope for minor queue containment" and "rescue footstool for minor queue
containment". Descriptor resources separately contain "predict minor queue
disputes", which reinforces the broader queue theme but is not the same gag.

The repetition is therefore principally B, generated ingredients, combined
with C, close semantic families. The provider can retain only the memorable
fragment or paraphrase it. Existing concept accepted-use tracking cools an
exact offered phrase and its structured premise when the full phrase survives.
It cannot reliably credit "seventeen accountants" after "clipboard haunted by
seventeen accountants" is shortened, or relate differently phrased queue-
containment prose. That accepted-output gap is the material cause.

No evidence showed A, a prompt example. D, independent provider invention,
cannot be excluded for unrecorded live outputs, but neither example requires it
to explain the observations. Phase 4G was functioning as designed: it targets
accepted descriptor mechanisms and name sound families and deliberately leaves
concept ordering unchanged after a broader penalty reduced quality.

Implementation
==============

`final_joke_memory.py` adds an explainable, rule-based accepted-output layer.
It currently recognizes two deliberately narrow construction families:

* numbered plural groups, normalizing "seventeen accountants", "a delegation
  of seventeen accountants" and "17 accountants" to one key;
* queue-containment scenarios, including emergency procedures and a specialist
  queue-containment unit.

The numbered-group detector keeps number and noun in its key, so it does not
ban accountants generally or every numbered-group joke. Queue containment has
one explicit scenario key; ordinary queues and unrelated bureaucracy remain
available. Constructions already present in the source article are exempt.

Before rewrite generation, evaluation mode makes one projected history read and
adds at most four recent labels to a concise `RECENT-JOKE VARIETY` instruction.
It asks for a fresh comic idea while explicitly permitting the broad subject.
There is no embedding, fuzzy service, extra model call, regeneration or
post-generation rewrite.

After `complete_draft` succeeds, an atomic article-sequence counter advances and
detected keys are upserted by `_id`. Failed, superseded or unpersisted rewrites
record nothing. The counter advances for every successful article, including
articles with no detected gag, so a construction expires after ten accepted
articles and can return. Concurrent increments and key updates are atomic.
Memory is advisory: read or write failure is logged only by exception class and
cannot block or discard a successful rewrite. Saturation changes guidance only
and cannot exhaust a generator, lease or quota.

The separate `NEWSMUNCHER_FINAL_JOKE_MEMORY` flag accepts `off` (default) or
`evaluation`. All existing vocabulary, descriptor and semantic-memory modes
remain independent.

Humour and naturalness
======================

The fixture's editorial quality proxy moves from 7.97 to 7.83 because a strong
but recently repeated gag is replaced by a slightly lower-scored, still curated
alternative. All fixture sentences pass the naturalness gate. These scores are
hand-authored proxies rather than reader ratings. They demonstrate the chosen
trade-off: stop an unmistakable gag recurring three times in five articles,
without penalizing accountants, queues, bureaucracy or broad comic mechanisms.

The implementation does not suppress every semantic similarity. New detector
families should be added only after an observed recurring final-output problem,
with explicit examples and source exemptions. This guards the humour loss seen
when Phase 4G experimented with more aggressive ranking.

Performance and prompt overhead
===============================

In the fixture, Phase 4H adds an average 237.58 characters, estimated at 59.39
tokens by the repository's conservative four-characters-per-token measure; the
maximum is 257 characters for the two active labels. It adds no provider call.
Local detection and guidance averaged about 0.01 ms in repeated runs.

The audit counts 542 final-memory operations across 250 successful rewrites:
one batched projected history read per rewrite, one atomic article-counter
write per persisted article and 42 detected-key upserts. That is 2.17 local
operations per rewrite in this fixture. Existing name, concept and descriptor
leases, cooldowns and writes are unchanged.

Tests and validation
====================

Focused tests cover:

* word and numeric forms of seventeen accountants;
* delegation wording and all requested queue-containment variants;
* broad queue references remaining unblocked;
* source-story exemption;
* cooldown expiry through neutral successful articles;
* default-off and invalid configuration;
* zero work while disabled;
* prompt injection without another provider call;
* recording after successful persistence only;
* failed rewrite isolation and graceful history-read failure;
* deterministic 250-MUNGE comparison and zero exhaustion.

Final validation results are recorded in CURRENT_WORK.md after the complete
Python, JavaScript, compilation, syntax and diff checks.

Manual evaluation
=================

Run the successful local vocabulary, descriptor and semantic-memory modes with
Phase 4H using:

  NEWSMUNCHER_EXPANDED_VOCABULARY=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_EXPANDED_DESCRIPTORS=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_COMEDY_SEMANTIC_MEMORY=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_FINAL_JOKE_MEMORY=evaluation \
  ./.venv/bin/python -m uvicorn newsmuncher.main:app --reload

Create at least twenty MUNGEs, including repeated MUNGEs from one source. Label
exact wording, close variants and broad-theme reuse separately. Confirm that a
source article containing a numbered group or queue-containment report can
retain that fact, failed MUNGEs do not change guidance, and both example gags
can return after ten other successful articles.

Remaining limitations
=====================

The two observed construction families are recognized with explicit rules.
Unseen paraphrases and unrelated recurring gags are not inferred. The numbered-
group rule may remember a mundane model-invented quantity, although source
facts are exempt and the consequence is only a temporary targeted prompt note.
The model can ignore negative guidance. Proving real reader-visible improvement
therefore requires the manual local review above; doing that automatically
would require paid calls or historical production data, neither used here.
