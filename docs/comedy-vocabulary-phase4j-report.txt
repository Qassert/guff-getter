NewsMuncher Phase 4J — evidence-based comedy variety improvements
Date: 2026-10-12

Scope and evidence
==================

This phase used the ignored local Phase 4I JSONL audit containing twenty real
completed MUNGEs. The audit was read locally and was not copied into tracked
fixtures, committed or uploaded. Tests and the 250-MUNGE evaluation use original
synthetic sentences only. No provider request or production database write was
made.

The implementation is independently gated by:

  NEWSMUNCHER_COMEDY_TOPIC_MEMORY=evaluation

The default is `off`. Existing prompts and selectors retain their Phase 4I
behavior unless that exact evaluation mode is enabled.

Confirmed causes of repeated offers
===================================

1. Offered-but-unused ingredients are intentionally excluded from accepted-use
   memory. Temporary leases are released after the completed rewrite. This is
   correct accounting, but it means a strong unused candidate can return in the
   next prompt.
2. Only two of 200 offered descriptors appeared as exact complete phrases in
   the twenty final articles, an average of 0.1 per article. Phase 4G therefore
   received almost no accepted descriptor evidence even though the model may
   have used fragments or paraphrases.
3. Quality and grammar ranking repeatedly exposed a small productive stratum.
   "Banned from speed topiary for wearing performance-enhancing brogues" was
   offered eight times; related county whispering and full-contact croquet forms
   were offered five and four times. Phase 4F diversification operates within
   grammar/category/intensity constraints and did not remember completed unused
   offers.
4. Concept exact strings were fairly varied, but bounded modifier families
   repeated. Premises for diplomatic immunity, tribunal self-representation,
   intercontinental pumping clearance and neighbour-started-it each appeared in
   six prompts. The bases ombudsman and advisory council also appeared six
   times. This is component concentration rather than an exact-lease failure.
5. Invented names were exact-unique in this sample, while seven final articles
   contained a Yaffle name. Phase 4G's broad consonant/vowel skeleton does not
   reliably isolate that memorable lexical root.
6. Accepted final memory and offered memory were independent by design. Phase
   4H saw only the explicit numbered-group and queue-containment rules. Only one
   canonical match was recorded, so illegal raffles, floorboards framing things,
   tribunal self-representation and swelling variants were invisible.

The registries were scoped and retrieved correctly. The repeated offers are
mostly an intentional memory boundary amplified by concentrated high-quality
candidates, rather than broken persistence or stale rewrite identities.

Source concentration
====================

Ten articles used the same baseball source, six the same song source, three the
same politician and one another source. That can repeat narrative shape and
factual vocabulary. The new final-topic detector subtracts any family already
present in the source before recording or prompting. The audited sources do not
by themselves explain the generated gravy, allotment, municipal, powered,
Yaffle and listed construction concentrations, but no claim is made that every
repeated word is generator-caused.

Implementation
==============

`topic_memory.py` stores one bounded `recent` document in the existing MongoDB
database. Each of its ten events keeps separate lists for completed offered
concept phrases, premises and bases; descriptor phrases and premises; name
families; and high-confidence final detections. One projected read supplies all
three selectors and prompt guidance. One atomic `$push`/`$slice` update records
the successful persisted rewrite. A rewrite-UUID condition prevents duplicate
events. Missing memory degrades to Phase 4I behavior.

The selection change is a soft ordering penalty:

* concepts rank recent completed phrase, premise, base and animal/object
  identities behind fresh eligible candidates;
* descriptors rank recent completed exact phrases and premises behind fresh
  candidates after all existing grammar, quota and eligibility checks;
* names retain Phase 4G sound-family memory and additionally distinguish the
  observed Yaffle root.

No completed offer is called accepted or written to accepted-use fields. Hard
exact cooldowns still apply only to accepted evidence. Active leases, atomic
acquisition, retry identity, category/intensity quotas, six descriptor roles,
quality floors and saturation behavior are unchanged. Recent-offer saturation
only changes order and cannot create exhaustion.

Final subject and construction recognition
==========================================

Deterministic detection now covers the evidence-led families:

* gravy; pudding/dessert; tea and tea equipment; allotments; milk floats;
  queueing; and municipal authority;
* X-powered-Y constructions;
* representing itself/himself/herself at a tribunal;
* running an illegal raffle across run/runs/running/ran;
* insisting that the neighbour started it;
* capable of unauthorised/unauthorized swelling;
* floorboard(s) framed it/them/him/her.

Each detection records a key, label, subject/construction kind and the exact
matched evidence. Phase 4I audits now expose these fields even when Phase 4J is
off, improving later diagnosis. Source matches are excluded. Broad words are
not hard-banned.

Prompt guidance appears only after one high-confidence family has occurred at
least twice within five completed articles. It contains at most four short
labels and explicitly asks for a different specific joke only when the source
does not require the recent subject. It preserves the same British absurdist
tone. There is no AI checker, embedding, regeneration or extra provider call.

Humour-quality risks
====================

The principal risk is steering away from a strong callback merely because it
appeared recently. Controls are deliberately mild: accepted material is not
banned, penalties expire after ten articles, prompt guidance requires two
appearances in five, source facts are exempt, and hard quality/grammar rules run
before diversity. The broad subject groups are few and evidence-led. They should
not be expanded from intuition alone.

Repeated final words can still be harmless or source-driven. Exact phrase
matching still misses paraphrases. The Yaffle special case is intentionally
narrow and should be generalized only when more real audit evidence supports a
safe name-root taxonomy.

Tests
=====

Focused coverage includes:

* all audited construction examples and tense/article variants;
* broad negative cases and source exemptions;
* gravy, dessert, tea, allotment, milk-float, queue and municipal subjects;
* completed-offer versus accepted-final separation;
* bounded history, duplicate rewrite identity and graceful missing memory;
* descriptor quality-floor preservation under an offer penalty;
* Yaffle root handling without suppressing unrelated names;
* route integration with one unchanged rewrite/provider flow;
* audit evidence/reason fields;
* deterministic 250-MUNGE metrics and the quality/exhaustion gates.

Full validation results are recorded in CURRENT_WORK.md.

Manual evaluation command
=========================

  NEWSMUNCHER_EXPANDED_VOCABULARY=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_EXPANDED_DESCRIPTORS=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_COMEDY_SEMANTIC_MEMORY=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_FINAL_JOKE_MEMORY=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_COMEDY_TOPIC_MEMORY=evaluation \
  NEWSMUNCHER_COMEDY_AUDIT_LOG=on \
  ./.venv/bin/python -m uvicorn newsmuncher.main:app --reload

Collect at least twenty new MUNGEs across several different sources. Compare
exact offers, partial uses and final topic detections separately, and read the
articles aloud. Keep Phase 4J evaluation-only unless the real audit confirms
that repetition falls without flattening the jokes.

Known limitations
=================

The offline provider response is synthetic. Real model uptake, paraphrasing and
humour cannot be proven without another live sample. Regex recognition favors
precision and will miss novel wording. Topic history is a bounded advisory
document rather than a complete analytics store; the Phase 4I JSONL remains the
authoritative diagnostic record. The Mongo update is atomic, but a process
crash after article persistence and before the advisory write can omit one
event; the saved article remains valid. No migration or historical backfill is
performed.
