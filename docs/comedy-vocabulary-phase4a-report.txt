NewsMuncher Phase 4A — universal comedy vocabulary expansion investigation
Date: 2026-10-10

Scope
=====

This phase audited the live/local vocabulary, researched redistribution-safe
sources and built a deterministic catalogue-selection prototype. The prototype
is `scripts/diagnostics/evaluate_vocabulary_expansion.py`; nothing imports it
from the application. No CSV, live generator, prompt, database or provider path
was changed.

Current inventory
=================

  names.csv                 428 /   423 / 5
  nouns.csv               1,710 / 1,710 / 0

Quantity is misleading. `animalsAndObjects.csv` combines taxonomy with domestic
objects and malformed compounds such as `tvremote`. `nouns.csv` is largely
already-composed rude phrases rather than reusable object identities. `adverbs`
mixes adjectives, participles, true adverbs and ordinary qualities. `slang`
contains grammatical roles, spelling errors, explicit phrases and discriminatory
slurs in one flat bank. `places` combines real geography, shops/institutions and
invented rude places. `names.csv` contains real/famous identities and remains a
legacy compatibility bank, not a component source.

Generated concept resources
----------------------------

Recognisable base banks: 70 domestic objects, 50 devices, 50 cheeky objects,
60 creatures, 40 bureaucratic topics, 30 equipment items, 40 ceremonial
objects and 30 emergency devices. There are 24 templates across seven
categories and 21 mapped comic mechanisms/themes.

The raw template-product estimate is 40,236 strings:

  everyday British 5,740; cheeky objects 3,900; absurd inventions 3,500;
  creatures 6,360; bureaucracy 17,496; emergency equipment 1,080;
  ceremonial objects 2,160.

The meaningful capacity is much lower. Several joke-bearing intensity banks
contain only 4–8 entries; most are 6–13. Thus thousands of lexical strings can
still be another powered appliance, sanctioned creature or council document.
Quality ranking further favours compact, visual, high-scoring mechanisms.

Generated descriptors
---------------------

There are 24 templates, 20 semantic mechanisms and all six grammatical roles.
The union of component values is 708. The raw independent slot-product is
about 9,265 strings. Older core banks hold 15–60 values; the eight Phase 3E
mechanisms commonly have 12. Meaningful situations are bounded more by subjects
and mechanisms than string permutations. Ranking favours 2–8-word phrases and
seven proven mechanisms, so high-scoring structures recur.

Invented names
--------------

Cheeky: 70 given names, 50 classic surnames, 46 roots, 40 suffixes, 15
nicknames, 32 curated names and six recipes. Absurd: 65 gendered given names,
40 classic surnames, 36 roots, 36 suffixes, 13 titles, 22 distinct epithets,
15 territories, 14 curated names and six non-phonetic recipes.

Raw capacity is enormous: cheeky constructed surnames alone yield 128,800
given/root/suffix strings; double barrels exceed 6.4 million. Yet spoken style
is dominated by 46 cheeky and 36 absurd root sounds, a small title set, and the
same recipe contours. Phase 3G fixes candidate exhaustion and exact-name reuse,
but cannot make those root families sound less familiar.

Selection and repetition
========================

Generated names, concepts and descriptors now use rewrite UUIDs, batched
availability reads, atomic final leases, accepted-use cooldowns and active
lease protection. Concepts remember exact phrase, base, premise and animal;
descriptors remember phrase, subject and premise; names remember exact name.
Unused offers are intentionally reusable. Concepts/descriptors may relax only
soft ingredient/premise history under demonstrated saturation; exact phrase
cooldown and active leases remain hard.

Remaining repetition is primarily editorial capacity rather than a registry
fault:

1. Physical bases are much smaller than raw phrase spaces.
2. Central joke-bearing modifier banks are smaller still.
3. Quality scoring repeatedly promotes reliable mechanisms.
4. Twenty concepts and ten descriptors are offered per rewrite, so even unused
   ingredients are visibly sampled quickly during sustained use.
5. Slang and place banks have no semantic/grammatical classification and use
   permanent legacy claims rather than recyclable structured memory.
6. Exact accepted matching cannot infer every model paraphrase without adding
   false claims or another model call.

Recommended catalogue sizes
===========================

These are curated post-filter targets, not raw download quotas:

  Animals:              1,500–3,000 common-name identities
  Physical objects:     6,000–10,000 classified identities
  Descriptor subjects:  2,000–4,000 across 25–40 comic situations
  Name given names:      800–1,500; surname/root units: 1,500–3,000
  Safe slang units:      1,000–2,500 role-labelled entries
  Place components:      2,000–5,000 stems/features/institutions

The animal target should include class/order/family metadata, familiarity and
novelty bands, scientific identity and one preferred English common name.
Objects need semantic affordances (wearable, powered, handheld, edible,
container, vehicle, ceremonial, medical, industrial). Descriptor ingredients
need grammatical role plus situation/mechanism metadata. Counts alone should
never be acceptance criteria.

Promising sources and licensing
===============================

Wikidata
--------

Structured item/property/lexeme data is CC0. It is the strongest common source
for taxon identities, English labels, subclass/category relations, object types,
occupations, title concepts and geographic feature types. Use versioned SPARQL
queries or dumps with QIDs retained for deduplication and provenance.
Automated access is permitted subject to endpoint etiquette; production should
consume a reviewed snapshot rather than query at runtime.

  https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
  https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:For_developers

Open English WordNet / Princeton WordNet
----------------------------------------

Open English WordNet provides noun, adjective, adverb and semantic relation
data under CC BY 4.0, incorporating Princeton WordNet material whose notice
must also be retained. Hypernym paths can separate artifacts, tools, clothing,
vehicles, foods, animals, traits and actions. It is suitable for producing
candidate lemmas, not automatic comedy approval. Preserve attribution, source
synset IDs and modification notices.

  https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet
  https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/blob/main/LICENSE.md
  https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use

GBIF / Catalogue-style taxonomy
-------------------------------

GBIF is valuable for accepted taxon IDs, rank and vernacular-name extensions,
but GBIF-hosted datasets can be CC0, CC BY or CC BY-NC individually. A blanket
redistributable import is unsafe. Only explicitly compatible datasets should be
selected, with dataset DOI/licence provenance and common-name normalization.
Scientific species alone are too obscure; score recognisability separately.

  https://www.gbif.org/terms
  https://docs.gbif.org/course-data-use/en/

OS Open Names and GeoNames
--------------------------

OS Open Names is available under the UK Open Government Licence and is best for
British settlement/road/feature morphology. GeoNames offers downloadable
worldwide features under CC BY 4.0 and requires attribution. Both describe real
places; use them to learn safe component morphology or preserve source facts,
not to silently replace essential real geography. Retain feature codes and
provenance.

  https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/documents/product-support/user-guide/os-open-names-user-guide.pdf
  https://www.geonames.org/export/
  https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/information-management/re-using-public-sector-information/uk-government-licensing-framework/open-government-licence/

ONS baby-name releases
----------------------

ONS publishes England/Wales names with counts below three suppressed for
confidentiality. Government material normally uses the OGL, but each downloaded
release should retain its explicit notice. These lists can broaden plausible
given-name eras/frequencies. They should not supply real full identities.

  https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/birthsdeathsandmarriages/livebirths/methodologies/babynamesqmi

Wiktionary
----------

Wiktionary can provide British usage labels and pronunciation clues, but entry
text is CC BY-SA 4.0/GFDL and may contain third-party material. Share-alike and
attribution make wholesale extraction less simple than CC0/CC BY sources.
Prefer a separate attributed derived dataset reviewed for compatibility, or
use Wikidata lexemes/OEWN first. Never ingest slang without slur, target-group,
explicitness, grammatical-role and regional review.

  https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights

Prototype experiment
====================

The offline prototype combines current structured banks with:

* 74 editorial animal identities spanning mammals, birds, reptiles,
  amphibians, fish, marine invertebrates, insects and arachnids;
* object-like entries conservatively filtered from the existing mixed legacy
  bank by physical suffix;
* simple adjective/adverb candidates from the existing descriptor CSV;
* 43 experimental British given/surname components;
* the full existing slang and place banks, explicitly marked unreviewed.

It models preloaded deterministic catalogues, recency rotation and production
offer counts for 100 rewrites. It performs zero database operations. It does
not claim that raw legacy additions are production quality.

Recommended architecture
========================

1. Build versioned offline catalogues, one row per identity/component with a
   stable source ID, preferred display form, aliases, licence/provenance,
   semantic class, grammatical metadata, recognisability, visual suitability,
   British relevance, comedy affordances and safety flags.
2. Compile reviewed source files into compact JSON/SQLite artifacts at build
   time. Load immutable indices once per process; do not query public datasets
   or Mongo per candidate.
3. Select category/semantic buckets deterministically from the rewrite UUID,
   then sample a broad local pool. Perform one batched usage-history read for
   identity/premise keys and lease only the final slate atomically.
4. Track accepted identity and premise keys, not every possible generated
   phrase. Preserve exact-phrase cooldown where the phrase exists, active lease
   ownership, retry stability and finite cooldown reuse.
5. Balance familiarity bands: typically 50–60% recognisable, 25–35% unusual but
   pronounceable, 10–15% properly obscure. Prevent taxonomic siblings or one
   equipment class from dominating a slate.
6. Treat template compatibility as typed constraints. Examples: a wearable can
   be ceremonially donned; a container can leak or contain; an animal can hold
   office; a medical device should not use food-only behavior without an
   intentional contrast rule.
7. Keep source places protected. Fictional place ingredients belong only in
   optional replacement roles explicitly permitted by the prompt.

Risks and limitations
=====================

* External labels contain aliases, spelling variants, taxonomic synonyms,
  trademarks, real people and offensive terms. Automated normalization cannot
  replace editorial review.
* Scientific catalogues optimize taxonomic completeness, not pronounceability,
  recognisability or comic usefulness.
* WordNet semantic classes help compatibility but senses are ambiguous and
  attribution must ship with derived data.
* Wikidata is CC0 but community data quality varies; retain QIDs and query date.
* CC BY/OGL imports require attribution; Wiktionary share-alike needs a specific
  repository licensing decision before redistribution.
* Bigger banks reduce lexical repetition but will not fix premise repetition
  unless mechanism/subject metadata expands in parallel.
* Preloading a reviewed 20,000-object catalogue is inexpensive (a few MiB), but
  large Python object graphs can multiply memory. Prefer compact records or
  SQLite indices and measure resident memory in the implementation phase.

Recommended phases
==================

4B — schema and acquisition tooling
  Define provenance/licence schema and deterministic import commands. Start
  with Wikidata CC0 animals and OEWN CC BY objects. Produce reports only.

4C — animal and object editorial pipeline
  Normalize aliases/taxonomy, classify affordances, score familiarity/visual
  value, create reject lists, and review at least 500 samples per category.

4D — descriptor and name components
  Expand joke-bearing situations and typed name components. Use ONS/OEWN only
  as raw plausible-language inputs; retain hand-authored comic mechanisms.

4E — slang and fictional places
  Build explicit role/safety registers and generative place morphology.
  Do not reuse the current flat CSV wholesale.

4F — shadow selection
  Run 1,000-rewrite current/expanded comparisons with rolling semantic reuse,
  compatibility rejection, memory, latency and concurrency tests.

4G — controlled live integration
  Introduce one catalogue at a time behind legacy/generated switches. Preserve
  provider count, prompt size, exact cooldowns, historical content and rollback.
